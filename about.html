THE IDEA BEHIND RAAD

MORE THAN
A CLIENT.

Raad is being built to bring performance, customization and a focused PvP experience together in one place.

OUR STORY

A better way to experience Minecraft.

Raad Client is an independent project with a clear goal: create a practical Minecraft launcher and a dedicated client experience, with special attention to PvP.

The project is under development. We are building its features step by step and want the experience to remain simple, customizable and accessible.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our principles

The ideas that guide the decisions behind Raad.

⚡

Performance

Keep the experience responsive and make efficient use of system resources.

🎯

Player-focused

Prioritize useful features and clear controls instead of unnecessary complexity.

🛠

Customization

Give players ways to personalize their interface and settings.
